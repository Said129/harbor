# Anime franchise graph and entry selection — work in progress

The reference is Desktop 0.9.132 (`0834340cec5531650bfe78cd628628702e636987`): `anime-detail.ts`, `anime-franchise-root.ts`, `anilist/relations.ts`, `anime-episodes/anime-season-picker.tsx` and the split-franchise policy in `anime-identity-core.ts`.

The native graph follows Kitsu sequel/prequel/parent-story relationships for three waves, adds the reference's AniList complement (six waves, checking its 40-node boundary before each wave), and checks the first two Kitsu siblings for missing AniList relations. It preserves source order during title deduplication, favors the current entry and Kitsu metadata, normalizes season/cour names, resolves known AniList aliases to real Kitsu IDs, and sorts chronologically. Movies, OVAs/ONAs, specials/music and eligible single-episode side entries retain their separate classification. Upcoming seasons are retained.

Root resolution walks at most eight prequel steps with a visited set, preferring series to movies, then year and ID as Desktop does. The Bleach root (`kitsu:244`) remains the only scoped split franchise; its separately opened parts use their own list and selector instead of being folded into the original root. No universal single-season rule is introduced.

The fallback season picker is used where the rich TVDB or ordinary provider-season picker is unavailable. It matches the reference's season tag, title, count, year and upcoming state, adapting the popover to a sheet on compact devices. It lists ordinary seasons; movies/specials remain graph entries for their own buckets rather than being mixed into that menu. Entry selection loads actual addon metadata, keeps the provider's video IDs, validates the returned identity, rejects stale owners/cancelled selections and resets old offers and episode state. The current metadata/related panels update in place. It does not synthesize playable episode IDs.

Public responses are validated and coalesced. The metadata gate permits at most four concurrent public requests, spaces AniList requests and reuses full Kitsu resources included in relationship responses. The in-memory response cache is bounded both by entry count and a 16 MiB accounting budget based on estimated JSON value cost. These are resource limits, not a physical-device performance measurement. Successful complete graphs are cached; incomplete traversals can recover without reloading their already successful responses. Public root identity is cached separately. Persistent root caching is not yet ported.

`AnimeEpisodeService` shares this verified public mapping cache; its existing canonical episode algorithm and raw stream/progress identifiers are retained. A Kitsu identity now survives subsequent optional TVDB still supplementation, including anime whose opening IMDb metadata lacks a country. No personal account credentials, configured addon URLs or keys enter the graph cache.

Nine new native cases cover protocol validation and concurrent bounds, traversal limits/cycles, prequel ranking, AniList aliases, partial recovery, title/extras/upcoming rules, actual entry selection/cancellation/owner checks, scoped Bleach selection and identity retention through TVDB. One new public contract checks actual Kitsu detail/relationships and their mapping. The existing real episode-99 adapter case is selected again specifically because its mapping-fetch producer changed. Other approved suites, the panel suite and IPA packaging are excluded from this scope.

Local inspection confirmed the two new Kitsu resource routes returned public JSON from the pinned endpoint, on the development host. Apple compilation, the selected native/public executions and the new fallback selector's visual/runtime review remain pending. The generated locales retain all existing labels and add reference-derived wording only. The repository checker cannot lint Swift/Python; no TypeScript or Rust product source changed.

The affected Apple selection was dispatched once as [39327069185](https://github.com/Said129/harbor/actions/runs/39327069185), freezing source `ee03d0a4b2fee30f9e9db9bb5a770e04c67f5dff`. Its nine new native cases, new public contract and affected mapping adapter are pending actual results. The independent episode-panel validation is retained on its own frozen source; neither scope packages an IPA or repeats unrelated approved suites.

This increment does **not** finish the franchise episode system. Still required are the full cross-entry episode pool; movie/special matching and source-aware playback, resume and watched routing; season-art changes driven by the TVDB section; foreign-season windows and remaining XML mapping fallbacks; persistent public root caching; and real iPhone/iPad UI/motion review. No profile/stream account synchronization is claimed here. The full Desktop port stays active and no IPA is produced until its complete agreed scope is integrated and validated.
